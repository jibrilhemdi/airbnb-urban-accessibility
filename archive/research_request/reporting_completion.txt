======================================================================
2. FINAL SCIENTIFIC DESIGN TO REPORT
======================================================================

The final thesis has FOUR research questions.

RQ1 — ACCESSIBILITY MEASUREMENT

How do Euclidean and walking-network measures characterize urban accessibility differently across Copenhagen, Oslo and Stockholm?

RQ2 — WITHIN-CITY PREDICTIVE CONTRIBUTION

How much predictive information do Euclidean and walking-network accessibility measures add to Airbnb asking-price models within each city?

RQ3 — GEOGRAPHIC GENERALIZATION AND CROSS-CITY CONSISTENCY

How robust are the predictive gains from accessibility when models are evaluated in geographically unseen neighbourhoods, and how consistent are those gains across Copenhagen, Oslo and Stockholm?

RQ4 — CROSS-CITY TRANSFERABILITY

To what extent do Airbnb pricing relationships learned in one city transfer to another city, and does accessibility improve this transferability?

Within-city outcome:

`log(original positive Airbnb nightly asking price)`

Cross-city outcome:

city-relative log price:

`log(price_ic) - mean_city(log(price))`

Cross-city transfer directions:

1. Copenhagen -> Oslo
2. Copenhagen -> Stockholm
3. Oslo -> Copenhagen
4. Oslo -> Stockholm
5. Stockholm -> Copenhagen
6. Stockholm -> Oslo

Primary model ladder:

- M0 = X
- ME = X + E
- MW = X + W
- MEW = X + E + W

Income is NOT in the active primary models.

Housing/property-price variables are NOT in the active primary models.

Use `Copenhagen` in active scientific writing after the one-time clarification that the study area includes Frederiksberg.
