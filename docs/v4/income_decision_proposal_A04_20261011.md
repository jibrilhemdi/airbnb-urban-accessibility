All are income measures rather than residential prices, but common labels do
not establish interchangeable definitions. Oslo's table documents assigning
income to each member, without proving the publication's mean-weighting matches
Copenhagen's decile construction. Sweden's scale and eligibility differ.
Currency rescaling cannot fix those differences. Median cannot replace mean.
The small Copenhagen municipal/national total discrepancy is inherited
supportive QA, not proof of three-city equivalence.

**Recommendation if a common concept cannot be qualified:** approve a common
no-income primary comparison in all three cities, with separately labelled
local-income models as sensitivities, preserving density/centrality and every
other inherited control. Keep identical samples/folds within each paired
comparison and disclose local concepts/scales/years. This would require a new
explicit pre-fit model-contract/registry amendment; A04 creates no alternative
primary model labels, changes no feature registry and does not approve this
recommendation. An alternative decision could authorize a substantively
justified common income construction with explicit source/eligibility/scaling
rules. Neither decision is implied by elapsed time or source acquisition.
